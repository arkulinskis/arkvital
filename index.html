My approach

Find the cause before choosing the fix.

A slow process could point to the wrong tool, a missing decision, or a team that has outgrown the way it works.

I’m Vitalijs: a mechanical and software engineer turned project and people leader. I work with the people closest to the problem, find the real source of friction, and help the team choose a change they can put into practice.

That may mean coaching a manager, building an internal tool, making business data useful, or automating a repetitive task. The solution should fit the problem.
