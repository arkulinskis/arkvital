01 / PEOPLE

Coaching and leadership

Help managers lead through change and build teams that work well together.

One-to-one coaching for managers working through growth, team dynamics, difficult conversations, or a new level of responsibility. Bring a real situation; leave with a clearer view of what to do next.

  • Leadership coaching and reflection
  • Team communication and working agreements
  • Support through change and uncertainty
02 / PRODUCT

Internal tools

Replace a clumsy workaround with a tool that fits the job.

I map the process with the people who use it, then prototype the smallest useful solution. It might be a lightweight app, a better workflow, or a clear product plan.

  • Workflow discovery and process mapping
  • Rapid prototypes and internal applications
  • Product thinking, requirements, and delivery
03 / INSIGHT

Data and analytics

Turn scattered numbers into a decision you can explain and act on.

Define the measures that matter, bring the relevant data together, and make the key signals clear to the people who need to decide what happens next.

  • Reporting and dashboard concepts
  • Metric definition and data exploration
  • Decision support for teams and leaders
04 / OPERATIONS

Automation

Automate repeatable steps; keep human judgment where it matters.

I look for repeated steps and unnecessary handoffs, simplify the process, then connect tools or automate the parts that don’t need a person in the loop.

  • Automation opportunity assessment
  • Process improvement and integrations
  • Human-friendly, maintainable solutions

A simple way to start

Listen. Plan. Build.

  1. Listen

    Understand the situation

    Tell me what’s happening, what you’ve tried, and what needs to change. We’ll see if I’m a fit to help.

  2. Plan

    Agree on the work

    For a larger challenge, a focused workshop with the right people turns the problem into priorities, deliverables, timing, and cost.

  3. Build

    Deliver in clear steps

    I do the hands-on work. You review progress and help steer decisions as we learn.

Start with a conversation

Let’s get to the source of it.

Tell me where the process, team, or tool is getting stuck. We’ll identify what to look at first.
